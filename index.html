Sauces

  • For the tuna pasta — Molho nata Mimosa para massas (€1.09). A ready-made cream sauce for pasta, no tomato. Warm it through with a little alho and azeite, then fold in the tuna and the cooked esparguete.
  • For the pork — Molho nata Mimosa para carne (€1.09). The Portuguese answer to gravy: a cream sauce made for meat, poured over the one-pot stew. If you'd rather make it from scratch, use the pan juices with a spoon of farinha.
  • Cream sauce (natas) — plain nata culinária, for the risotto, the creme de legumes and the bacalhau com natas.
  • Béchamel — made at home: farinha whisked into warm leite with manteiga.
  • Tomato sauce — homemade from tomate triturado, onion and azeite. For the tomato soup and the tomato rice only.

How the week is built

  • No onion in the pasta. Onion appears only as a frying base — softened first in the rice, the stew and the soups. Neither massa dish contains onion.
  • Tuna pasta gets a cream sauce, never tomato — the tuna is only ever paired with oil, garlic or molho de natas.
  • Soups are only tomato, vegetables or carrot. No bean, kale or mixed soups: sopa de legumes, sopa de tomate, creme de legumes and creme de cenoura.
  • No milk as a drink. Breakfast is served with iogurte líquido; lunch and dinner come with água, and three meals get a refrigerante. Milk appears only as an ingredient — in the cereal, the béchamel and the creme de legumes.
  • Breakfasts stay light — only three of seven use bread (two tostas and a sandes); the rest are iogurte with muesli or cereal with milk and fruit.

How the prices work

  • Each meal's price is its share of the shopping list: every purchase is divided across the meals that use it, so the 21 meals and 7 snacks add up to the €65.37 food total.
  • Delivery (€1.49) is not included in the meal prices; it's on the order total instead.
  • Prices are live from the Glovo Auchan catalogue and can change; fresh produce and meat are sold by weight, so the final bill may differ by a few cents.
  • The catalogue has no plain milk, so lactose-free milk is used. Your ticks are saved in this browser.
